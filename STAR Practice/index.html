Practice your STAR answers

Write your answer to each question. Then check it. The checker looks for Situation, Task, Action and Result. It also flags common problems.

These are sample questions. Not every interview asks these exact questions. They are common, so practicing them builds stories you can reshape for other questions.

The STAR method

S SituationSet the scene in one or two sentences.
T TaskSay what you needed to do.
A ActionWalk through the steps you took. Spend the most time here.
R ResultSay what changed. Add a number and a lesson.
